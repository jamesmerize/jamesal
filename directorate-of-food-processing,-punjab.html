Schemes & Subsidies

Comprehensive support for food processing enterprises – from micro units to large industries

Central Scheme

PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)

Credit-linked subsidy up to ₹10 Lakhs for micro food processing units. Covers upgradation of equipment, packaging, quality standards & marketing.

35% credit-linked capital subsidy Eligible: SHGs, FPOs, Cooperatives Seed capital for SHG members
Apply Now
Central Scheme

Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Food Processing

Incentivise manufacturing of food products with global standards. PLI rates of 4–10% on incremental sales for 6 years. Supports large & mid-scale food enterprises.

4% to 10% PLI on incremental sales Minimum investment ₹10 Cr Applicable for 6 years
Know More
Central Scheme

Integrated Cold Chain & Value Addition Infrastructure

Financial assistance for creation of integrated cold chain infrastructure without any break from farm gate to consumer. Covers pre-cooling, cold storage, IQF, CA stores.

Grant up to ₹10 Cr per project 35% of eligible project cost Farmer Producers priority
Apply Now
Central Scheme

Mega Food Park Scheme

Create modern infrastructure facilities for food processing in close proximity to raw material sources. Phagwara Mega Food Park – plots available for allotment.

Grant up to ₹50 Crore per park Common processing facilities 30+ food processing units
Allotment Details
State Scheme

Punjab State Capital Subsidy Scheme for Food Processing

State government subsidy for new food processing units and technology upgradation. Additional top-up on central schemes for Punjab-domiciled entrepreneurs.

15% additional state subsidy SC/Women entrepreneurs – extra 5% MSME units priority
Apply Now
New Scheme

One District One Product (ODOP) – Food Processing

Promote speciality food products unique to each of Punjab's 23 districts. Special branding, marketing support and GI tagging assistance for traditional food products.

23 districts covered GI tagging support National & export market linkage

Who We Are

Directorate of Food Processing, Punjab

Department of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare, Government of Punjab

The Directorate of Food Processing, Punjab (DFPP) is a nodal agency under the Department of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare, Government of Punjab. Established to boost the food processing industry in the state, DFPP works towards creating a robust ecosystem for food processing enterprises ranging from micro-level units to large industries.

Punjab, the food bowl of India, produces a significant share of the nation's wheat, rice, and dairy. DFPP harnesses this agricultural strength to build a vibrant food processing sector that adds value to farm produce, reduces post-harvest losses, and generates employment for rural communities.

We serve as the single-window facilitator for all central and state government schemes related to food processing, connecting entrepreneurs with financial assistance, technology, infrastructure, and market access.

Single Window Facilitation for all food processing schemes
Nodal agency for PMFME & PLI Schemes
Cold Chain & Food Park Infrastructure development
Skill development & entrepreneurship support
Promotion of GI-tagged Punjab food products
Export market linkage for food enterprises
About the Directorate Org. Structure

Our Vision

To make Punjab a leading state in food processing by 2030 — creating a world-class, technology-driven food value chain that empowers farmers, entrepreneurs, and workers while positioning Punjab's food products globally.

Our Mission

  • Promote and support food processing investments in Punjab
  • Reduce post-harvest losses through cold chain infrastructure
  • Facilitate credit, subsidy & technical support for food enterprises
  • Develop skilled human resource for the food processing sector
  • Enhance farmer income through better market linkages

Punjab Food Economy

Punjab contributes ~18% of India's wheat and ~11% of rice production. The state has immense potential to become a global food processing hub with targeted investment & policy support.
